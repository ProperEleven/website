Stoppy

A 3D puzzle game for iPhone, iPad and Android

Free, with no ads and no in-app purchases

Think first, then swipe

Swipe, and Stoppy rolls until something stops him. He can't stop halfway, so before each move you work out where he will end up.

Get him to the glowing finish in as few moves as you can. The fewest moves earn three stars. Mind the potholes: each fall costs a life, and you have three per level.

Every level is a small logic puzzle with no dead ends, so wherever you've rolled, you can still win. The first level shows each move before you make it.

Five worlds, 50 levels

Each world adds something new. They open one after another, with ten levels in each.

  1. Paint Yard

    Roll through paint to take its colour. Jelly gates only let their own colour through, and water washes the paint off. Red is heavy: a red ball smashes cracked blocks.

  2. Bounce Street

    Blue is bouncy, so a blue ball hops over hurdles. Red and blue make purple, which does both.

  3. Uptown

    The boards rise in platforms. Stairs are the only way up, and rolling off an edge drops him a floor.

  4. The Docks

    Grab the glove to shove wooden crates. A crate slides until something stops it, and one pushed into a pothole fills it.

  5. Prickle Park

    Spiky Prickles roll around on their own. Touch one and you lose a life, so wait for a gap.

More to play

The daily puzzle and challenges open once you finish the first world, Paint Yard.

Good to know

Price
Free. There are no ads and nothing to buy.
Internet
Stoppy plays offline, so you don't need Wi-Fi.
Sign-in
None. Your progress stays on your phone or tablet.
Hints
You earn points as you play, and points pay for hints that show your next move. You also get three undos a game.
Controls
Swipe, or turn on the arrow buttons in Settings and tap.
Age rating
4+ on the App Store. On Google Play, it's rated Everyone in the US and 3+ in India.
Needs
iOS 15, iPadOS 15 or Android 7.0, or later.
Language
English.

Get Stoppy

It's free on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, and on Google Play for Android.
