Stoppy privacy policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

Stoppy is a puzzle game made by Proper Eleven Technologies Private Limited ("we", "us"). This page explains what happens to your information when you play Stoppy on iPhone, iPad, Android or in a web browser.

In short: Stoppy doesn't collect, keep or share any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers, and your progress stays on your device.

What the game saves, and where

Stoppy saves your progress on your own device: the levels you've finished and their stars, your points and awards, your daily puzzle streak, your challenge records, what Stoppy is wearing, and your settings. The iPhone, iPad and Android apps keep this in the app's own storage. In a web browser, it's kept in that browser's local storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

To delete it, use Settings › Reset progress in the game (your settings stay as they are), delete the app, or clear the site's data in your browser.

What Stoppy doesn't do

  • No sign-in or accounts.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Sharing a daily result

When you tap Share after a daily puzzle, Stoppy passes a short line of text, with the puzzle number, your stars, your moves and your streak, to your device's share sheet, or copies it to the clipboard. You choose where it goes. We don't see it or receive it.

Vibration

On phones, Stoppy vibrates for feedback. This needs no special permission and collects nothing. You can turn it off in Settings.

Playing in a web browser

If you play Stoppy in a browser, the website that serves the game may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and browser types, to run and protect the site. If you play on another website, such as a game portal, that site's own privacy policy also applies.

Children

Stoppy is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If Stoppy ever changes how it handles information, for example by adding ads, we'll update this page and the date at the top before that change reaches the game.
