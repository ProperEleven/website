Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. Stoppy has no accounts and doesn't send your progress anywhere, so it isn't shared between devices. If you delete the game, your progress goes with it.

How do I start again from the beginning?

Tap the gear on the title screen to open Settings, then tap Reset progress. The game asks before it clears anything. Your sound and vibration settings stay as they are.

Can I play without swiping?

Yes. In Settings, turn on Arrow buttons. Four arrows appear at the bottom of the screen for you to tap, and swiping still works.

I'm stuck on a level. What can I do?

Tap Hint to see your next move. Hints cost points, which you earn from stars, awards, the daily puzzle and challenges. You also get three undos in each game.

Does Stoppy have ads or purchases?

No. Stoppy is free, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no sign-in.
